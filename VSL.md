# Vidflow VSL — voice-over script (≈60 s)

The landing page plays a motion-design VSL built in code (components/landing/vsl.tsx).
Record this voice-over over a screen capture of it, export as `public/vsl.mp4`, and the page plays your video instead.

| # | Scene (on screen) | Voice-over | ≈ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A 62-minute waveform fills up | "You recorded a one-hour podcast. Somewhere in there are thirty moments that could go viral." | 8 s |
| 2 | "6h" counter, tasks crossed out | "But cutting it into TikToks? Scrubbing for hooks, typing captions, hunting b-roll… that's your whole weekend. Gone." | 9 s |
| 3 | A link typed into Vidflow, "Find my clips" pressed | "So here's what I do now. I paste one link into Vidflow. That's it." | 9 s |
| 4 | Percentage climbing, cut points popping with scores | "The AI watches every second, reads the transcript, and scores every moment for how hard it hooks." | 10 s |
| 5 | Three phones with kinetic captions, "30 clips ready · 1:58" | "Two minutes later: thirty faceless clips. Kinetic captions, b-roll, vertical, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts." | 10 s |
| 6 | A week of checkmarks, views counting up | "Now I post every single day — and I don't edit anything." | 9 s |
| 7 | "Vidflow." + "Find my clips" button | "Paste your video. Get your first clips free. Vidflow." | 8 s |

Tone: calm, confident, a creator talking to another creator. No hype words.
